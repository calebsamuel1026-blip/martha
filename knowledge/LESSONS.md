# Martha's lessons (newest first)

What real results taught Martha. Lessons override PLAYBOOK.md when they conflict, so only add a lesson you can back with evidence. This file ships with a few starter lessons that hold for anyone; replace and extend them with your own.

## Format

One bullet per lesson, newest at the top:

```
- YYYY-MM-DD [source] [project] [channel] [evidence] <the rule, one or two sentences>
```

- `source`: `outcome` (real sends and replies), `ruling` (the owner decided it), `eval:<cycle id>` (practice evidence from IMPROVE.md), or `research:<id>` (a rule id from RESEARCH-EVIDENCE.md).
- `evidence`: counts and refs, never personal data. Example: `n=40 vs 40 sends, 3 vs 0 replies` or `train avg 2.6 on curiosity`. Mark eval-derived lessons as practice evidence; real outcomes (ideally 30+ sends per group) override them.
- Keep names, emails, phone numbers and company names of prospects out of this file. Refer to messages by an id only.

Example (fictional):
```
- 2026-01-15 [outcome] [brightpane] [cold_email] [n=60 vs 60, 4 vs 1 human replies] Opening on the storefront's streaky glass beat opening on our price. Lead with what the owner can see.
```

## Starter lessons (general, practice evidence)

- [ruling] COUNT, don't estimate. Run a word or character count on the final text before returning. Defaults: cold email 80 words target, 110 hard cap; spoken or chat turns 60 words or less; SMS 160 characters or less; LinkedIn connection note 200 characters or less. Any limit in the task is a hard cap.
- [ruling] Never work around the pre-return gate. If a fact is unknown, keep the visible placeholder and tell the caller what to fill. An unfilled blank reaching a real person is worse than a delayed draft.
- [ruling] Sell the outcome, not the product. Open a cold email on what they get (jobs, time, money back), then the specific gap they'd recognize as the "how", then price or terms per your rules, then one CTA. Never name tools or mechanisms they didn't ask about.
- [ruling] No client stories in a cold email or first DM. Proof belongs on the site and the call, and only if it is real and allowed in the project CONTEXT.
- [eval] Every outbound message needs one short, open question about their situation (how or what, not yes or no), on its own line before the CTA. Drafts without one score low on curiosity.
- [eval] No colon-reveal openers ("Straight answer:", "Here's the thing:") and no "not X, it's Y" lines. They read as AI.
- [eval] No unsourced generalizations about their industry ("most companies in your trade..."). Speak about this prospect's facts or your own offer only.
- [eval] Money lines keep "could" and "about". Never show a math chain; never invent a number by hand.
- [eval] Close every turn with ONE next step written in the message itself (a day and time, or a single action), never only in notes.
- [eval] Never use a discount deadline to push someone who already booked. Urgency only before the booking, and only a real deadline.
- [eval] Always state the term next to the price, and say plainly what is not included when asked.
- [eval] Final or exit touches must still pass the swap test and give one clear exit, not two.
- [eval] Never overstate the reader's interest or details beyond what their own page or message says.
- [eval] When a template is filled for many prospects, read every rendered version, not the template. Grammar and trade words break in ways the template hides.
